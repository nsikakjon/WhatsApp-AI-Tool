The service

yarnOga provides WhatsApp assistants run by Kahn Digital Company Ltd (RC 1601716), Lagos: Quotes & Orders and Birthday Flyers. They run on Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, so Meta's own WhatsApp Business Policy applies too.

Plans and payment

  • Prices are shown in naira on this site and in the chat. Plans are paid in advance through Paystack, by the month or the year, and run until the date shown when you pay.
  • When a paid plan ends and isn't renewed, your account moves back to the free plan. Nothing is deleted.
  • Payments for time already started are not refunded, except where the law says otherwise or we could not provide the service.
  • Meta charges for WhatsApp messages beyond its free allowance. Those charges are separate from our plan prices, and for a number you connect yourself, Meta bills you directly.
  • We may change prices. A change never affects time you have already paid for, and we announce it before it applies.

Your prices and your customers

  • Your sales are between you and your customer. Money paid through a yarnOga pay link goes to your account through Paystack (naira) or Flutterwave (CFA francs), less that provider's fee and yarnOga's fee of 2.5% of each payment (at most ₦2,000, or 800 FCFA). Refunds and disputes on those payments are handled with us and the provider.

Don't use yarnOga to send spam, to mislead or harass anyone, to sell anything illegal or anything WhatsApp's policies forbid, or for political campaigns. Don't try to break, overload or copy the service. We may pause or close an account that does, and we will tell you why.

Meta can block a number that does.

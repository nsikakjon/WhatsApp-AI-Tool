How it works

Set up once, greet everyone all year.

  1. Send your group logo

    Chat with yarnOga on WhatsApp and pick a design and colour.

  2. Add your members

    Send each member's name, birthday and photo, or import the whole list from a spreadsheet.

  3. Forward on the day

    Every birthday morning you get the flyer and a message. Long-press, forward to the group, tag the celebrant.

Questions

Things admins ask us.

Do my members need to install anything?

No. Everything happens inside WhatsApp. Only the admin chats with yarnOga.

Does it post in my group by itself?

No. WhatsApp doesn't let assistants join ordinary groups, so it sends the flyer to you and you forward it. You stay in control of your group.

What if a member's photo is dark or too far away?

Every photo is touched up when it arrives: framed on the face and the light fixed. If you want it different, tap Edit photo and pick brighter, darker, warmer, closer, plain background, black and white, rotate or original.

How do I pay for a plan?

Send upgrade standard, upgrade premium or upgrade deluxe in the chat. You get a Paystack link (card, bank transfer or USSD) and your plan switches on as soon as you pay. Pay for 1 to 12 months at a time, for example upgrade standard 3 months.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new group gets all Standard features free for 7 days or 30 flyers, whichever comes first. No card needed. Afterwards your group carries on free on the Free plan, and nothing is deleted.

Which designs are on the free plan?

Four: two classic designs plus minimal cream and retro sunburst. Standard unlocks 23 in any colour, Premium 26, and Deluxe all 28.

Data deletion

How to delete your data.

You can ask us to delete everything we hold about you at any time. It is free, and you don't have to give a reason.

Last updated 23 October 2026

  1. How to ask
  2. What we delete
  3. How long it takes
  4. If you chatted with a business
  5. Facebook and Meta

How to ask

  1. Email support@yarnoga.com with the subject Delete my data.
  2. Include the WhatsApp number you used (or your WhatsApp username, if your number is hidden) and which yarnOga service or business you chatted with.
  3. We send a WhatsApp message to that number to confirm the request came from you.

Email a deletion request

What we delete

Your messages and our replies, voice notes, photos, quotes, orders, delivery addresses, and flyer details linked to your number, across every yarnOga assistant. If you run a business or group on yarnOga, we also delete its price list, photos, members and settings.

Payment records the law makes us keep are kept until the law allows us to delete them, and then deleted. Paystack keeps its own records under its own policy.

How long it takes

We confirm your request within 3 working days and finish deleting within 30 days. We tell you on WhatsApp or by email when it's done.

If you chatted with a business

If you chatted with a shop or group that uses yarnOga, you can ask that business or ask us directly. We will delete your data and let the business know.

Facebook and Meta

yarnOga does not use Facebook Login for customers. If you connected a business WhatsApp number to yarnOga through Meta, you can also remove yarnOga's access in your Meta Business settings, and then ask us to delete the data as above.

    What is free

    Messages your customers send you cost nothing.

    Meta only charges for some messages the business sends. A business that mostly replies to customers who message first pays far less than one that sends lots of promotions.

    From 1 October 2026 Meta started charging for some replies inside the 24-hour customer service window. That is what the "charged service replies" row is for; if you are not sure, leave it at 0 and check the rate card.

    The four types

    What each message type means.

    • Marketing: promotions, new arrivals, festive greetings, anything that sells.
    • Utility: updates about something the customer already did, like an order confirmation or delivery update.
    • Authentication: one-time passwords and login codes.
    • Service: replies to a customer who messaged you.

    Questions

    Things people ask us.

    Do customers' messages to me cost anything?

    No. Messages your customers send to your business are free. Meta charges the business for some messages the business sends, by type.

    Why are the rates in dollars?

    Meta publishes its Nigeria rates in US dollars (and in a few other billing currencies). What you pay in naira depends on the exchange rate your bank or provider uses, so type in today's rate.

    Are these figures exact?

    No, they are an estimate. The default rates are the Nigeria figures reported from 1 October 2026; some providers list slightly different numbers, and Meta changes its rates from time to time. Always check Meta's official rate card.

    Does the WhatsApp Business app charge per message?

    No. The free WhatsApp Business app on your phone has no per-message fees. These rates apply to the WhatsApp Business Platform (the API), which automated assistants run on.

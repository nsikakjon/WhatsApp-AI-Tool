Questions

Things owners ask us.

Will it ever give a wrong price?

No. Every price and total is worked out from your own list exactly as you set it, including bulk prices. If an item isn't on the list, the chat comes to you.

Do I need a website?

No. Customers ask, order and pay inside WhatsApp. If you want a page to share, send shop to your bot and you get one, like app.yarnoga.com/shop/your-name, with your products and prices. Put it in your Instagram bio or WhatsApp status. Already have a website? Point its WhatsApp button at your bot’s number.

Is the Demo Shop a real shop?

No. The yarnOga Demo Shop is made up, with a small sample price list, so you can try the assistant. Orders there are pretend and nothing is charged. Your assistant uses your own prices, name and number.

How do my customers pay?

With pay links switched on, every invoice carries a link to pay. In naira it is Paystack: card, bank transfer or USSD, less Paystack's fee (1.5% + ₦100, at most ₦2,000). In cedis, CFA francs, shillings, Rwandan francs or kwacha it is Flutterwave: Mobile Money, M-Pesa or card, less Flutterwave's fee. yarnOga keeps 2.5% of each pay-link payment, at most ₦2,000 or about the same in your currency (GH₵20, 800 FCFA, K35, KSh 200). The money goes straight to your own account, the order is marked paid and the customer gets a receipt. Customers can still pay into your account directly, and those payments carry no yarnOga fee.

Can it arrange delivery?

Yes, on Pro and above. Delivery fees come from your own zones, your riders get the job on WhatsApp, and the customer can follow the delivery.

Will it message my customers too often?

No. Payment reminders stop at two and never go to anyone who replied STOP, and the rating question comes once per paid order. Nothing goes out while your staff have taken over a chat. Messages only go within WhatsApp’s 24-hour window after the customer last wrote; once that closes, the message is skipped. You can switch reminders, ratings and the morning summary off in your settings.

Can I keep my WhatsApp number?

Yes. If your number is on the WhatsApp Business app, connect it with Facebook and keep using the app on your phone: the assistant answers alongside you and steps back in a chat when you reply yourself. Or use a new SIM just for orders, and we connect it for you.

What if my prices change?

Send prices to your bot and change them in the chat, or use your setup screen. The next quote uses the new price.

Can I use it at my shop counter too?

Yes. Every plan can record counter sales, so walk-in and chat sales add up in one place. From Starter, scan barcodes and keep selling at the till when the internet drops; the sales go through once you are back online.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Up to 50 orders and 500 AI replies a month, with a small “Powered by yarnOga” line under quotes. Send upgrade starter to your bot when you outgrow it.

Do I need to know technology?

No. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can set it up: send sign up, then your prices, and check them before anything goes live. On Growth and Enterprise we set it up for you.

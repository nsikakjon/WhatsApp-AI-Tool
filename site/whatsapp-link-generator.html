Questions

Things people ask us.

Is the WhatsApp link generator free?

Yes. It is free with no sign-up. The link and QR code are made in your browser and nothing you type is sent to us.

How do I write my number?

Pick your country and type the number the way you normally would, like 0803 123 4567 in Nigeria or 0712 345 678 in Kenya. We turn it into the international format WhatsApp needs (2348031234567). If your country is not in the list, choose Other and type the full number with its country code.

Does the QR code expire?

No. The QR code just holds your wa.me link, so it works for as long as your number is on WhatsApp.

Can a bot answer the people who tap my link?

Yes. A yarnOga assistant can answer them day and night: give prices from your list, take orders and pass serious buyers to you.

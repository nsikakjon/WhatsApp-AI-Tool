Questions

WhatsApp AI assistants, answered.

New to this? Read our plain guide to WhatsApp AI assistants.

What is a WhatsApp AI assistant?

A chatbot that replies to your customers on your business WhatsApp number, day and night. It understands normal messages and voice notes, then gives prices, takes orders and sends invoices, or makes birthday flyers for your group.

Which countries does it work in?

yarnOga is built in Lagos and works with businesses in Nigeria, Ghana, Zambia, East Africa (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and Malawi) and the CFA franc countries of West and Central Africa, such as Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal and Cameroon. Prices come from your own list in your own currency, never guessed by the AI. In Nigeria customers pay through Paystack by card, bank transfer or USSD. Everywhere else they pay through Flutterwave by Mobile Money, M-Pesa or card, and you pay for your plan in your own currency too. Elsewhere in Africa? Write to us.

Can it understand voice notes, Pidgin, Hausa, Swahili and French?

Yes. Customers can type or send a voice note in English, Nigerian Pidgin, Hausa, Swahili or French, and the reply comes back in the language they wrote in.

Can I keep my WhatsApp number?

Yes. If your number is on the WhatsApp Business app, connect it with Facebook and keep using the app on your phone: the assistant answers alongside you and steps back in a chat when you reply yourself. Or use a new SIM just for orders, and we connect it for you.

How much does it cost?

Quotes & Orders has a free plan, and paid plans start at ₦7,000 a month. Birthday Flyers are free for groups of up to 10 members.

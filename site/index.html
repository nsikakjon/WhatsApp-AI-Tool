Questions

Before you book.

Is this a chatbot?

It answers on WhatsApp, but the work happens behind the chat. Every enquiry becomes a quote from your own price list, then a booking or order, an invoice, a payment matched to that order and a line in tomorrow's report. Your front desk and back office, open all night.

What happens in the walkthrough?

Twenty minutes on a call, or at your office in Lagos. We look at how enquiries reach you today and show the assistant quoting from a few of your own items. You leave knowing what it would do for your team, what it costs and when it could go live.

Will it ever give a wrong price?

No. The AI understands what the customer is asking for. Every price, total, deposit and delivery fee is worked out from your own list, never by the AI. If an item is not on the list, the chat comes to your staff.

Can we keep our WhatsApp number?

Yes. Connect the number you already use through Meta's own window and keep the WhatsApp Business app on your phones. The assistant answers alongside your staff and steps back in a chat when one of them replies.

How much does it cost?

Plans for teams are Growth at ₦73,000 a month and Enterprise at ₦298,000 a month, and on both we set it up for you. After the walkthrough you get the full price in writing, before anything starts. Running a small shop? The free plan and Starter are on the small shops page.

Who owns our customers and records?

You do. We use your data only to run your assistant, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. Our privacy policy lists every service that handles it.

Automatic birthday flyers for your WhatsApp group.

If you run a church, alumni, office or family WhatsApp group, you know the pressure: someone's birthday, no flyer, and the admin is blamed. Here is how to set it up once and get a finished flyer every birthday morning, ready to forward.

Read the steps Start free on WhatsApp

Set it up in five steps

  1. Message the flyer bot

    Send "hi" to yarnOga Flyers on +234 704 385 7494. Nothing to install. Only the admin chats with the bot; your members don't need to do anything.

  2. Name your group and send your logo

    Reply with the name as it should appear on flyers, for example Unilag Class of 2010. Then send your logo as a picture (or reply skip).

  3. Pick a design

    The bot shows the designs with your group's name already on them. Tap one. You can change it any time by sending design.

  4. Add your members

    Send each member's photo with a caption like add Ada Okafor, 14 March. For a big group, paste a Google Sheets link or send a CSV file with columns Name, Birthday and Photo.

  5. Forward on the morning

    On each birthday morning the bot sends you the flyer and a short birthday message. Long-press, forward to the group, then type @ and pick the person's name to tag them.

Collecting birthdays without stress

Make a Google Form with three questions: full name, birthday (day and month only) and a photo. Share it in the group. The answers land in a Google Sheet; rename the columns to Name, Birthday and Photo, set sharing to "Anyone with the link", and paste the link to the bot.

What it costs

Event flyers too

On Standard and above, send any photo with text in the caption, for example Homecoming: Sat 12 Dec, 4pm, Alumni Hall, and it comes back as a branded flyer with your logo. Useful for meetings, thanksgiving services, send-forths and anniversaries.

Questions

Common questions.

Does the bot post in my group by itself?

No. It sends the flyer to you and you forward it, so you stay in control of your group.

Do members need to install anything?

No. Everything happens inside WhatsApp, and only the admin chats with the bot.

Can two people manage the group?

Yes. Send add admin and their number, for example add admin 08012345678. Free allows 1 admin, Standard 2, Premium 5.

What if a member has no photo?

Their flyer shows their initials until you send a photo captioned photo and their name.

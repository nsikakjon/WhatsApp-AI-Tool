Who we are

yarnOga is run by YarnOga Africa Ventures, a business name registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (BN 9980032), Lekki, Lagos, Nigeria. Contact us about anything on this page at support@yarnoga.com.

Two kinds of people use yarnOga. Businesses and group admins sign up to use an assistant. Their customers, members and staff chat with that assistant. For the second group, the business or group that set up the assistant decides what it is used for, and we handle the data on its behalf.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp details: your WhatsApp number (or username, if your number is hidden) and the name on your WhatsApp profile.
  • What you send: messages, voice notes, photos, documents and locations you send to a yarnOga number, and our replies.
  • Orders and quotes: what you asked for, prices, totals, order numbers and delivery addresses.
  • Payments: the amount, reference and status Paystack or Flutterwave sends us. Card, bank and Mobile Money details are entered on their pages and never reach us.
  • Birthday flyers: the names, birthdays and photos a group admin adds for members, and the group's logo.
  • Riders: while a rider keeps their delivery page open, its position, so the customer can follow the delivery.
  • Business details: for businesses on the quote bot, the business name, price list, product photos, stock levels, plan, the Paystack or Flutterwave subaccount that receives its money, and the email address the owner gives us for receipts and updates.

How we use it

  • To answer your messages and do what you asked: give a quote, take an order, make a flyer or pass your message to the right person.
  • To send invoices, receipts, delivery updates and, where a business has them switched on, one reminder about a quote you haven't answered.
  • To take payments for plans and orders through Paystack (naira and US dollars) and Flutterwave (other African currencies).
  • To keep the service working and safe: fixing faults, stopping abuse and counting usage against plan limits.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

AI and voice notes

Our assistants use AI to understand messages. Message text and voice notes are processed by AI providers we contract with. They process it only to give us an answer and, under our agreements with them, do not use it to train their models. The AI never sets a price: prices and totals are worked out from the business's own price list.

Who helps us run yarnOga

We use these services, each only for its part of the job:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): carries the messages.
  • Cloud hosting and database providers: run our servers and store our data and files.
  • AI providers: read messages and write out voice notes, as above.
  • Paystack and Flutterwave: payments. Paystack takes naira and US dollar payments; Flutterwave takes payments in Ghana cedis, CFA francs, Kenyan, Ugandan and Tanzanian shillings, Rwandan francs and Zambian and Malawian kwacha, by Mobile Money, M-Pesa or card.
  • An email service: sends our emails to business owners.
  • A font service: loads the fonts on yarnoga.com.
  • Google Analytics: counts visits to yarnoga.com, as below.

To get the names of the companies in each group, email support@yarnoga.com.

Some of these services store data outside Nigeria. We only use providers that protect it to a standard the Nigeria Data Protection Act accepts. We also share data with the business or group whose assistant you chatted with, and with the authorities if the law requires it.

Our website

yarnoga.com uses no advertising trackers. Two tools count visits. Our own small counter records which page was opened and the page you came from; to count visitors per day it makes a code from your internet address and browser that changes every day, and the address itself is not kept. We also use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to tell visits apart and shows us which pages people open, how they found the site and roughly which country they are in. Google processes this for us, and we have switched off its advertising features. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with Google's opt-out add-on. If you use the free reply generator, the description you type is sent to our server and to an AI provider to write the replies, and is not kept afterwards.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while the business or group you dealt with uses yarnOga, or until you ask us to delete it. Records the law makes us keep, such as payment records, are kept for as long as the law requires and then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, or object to how we use it. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. To delete your data, see how to delete your data. For anything else, email support@yarnoga.com.

If you chatted with a business's assistant, you can also ask that business; we will help it answer.

Keeping it safe

Data travels encrypted, our storage is private, and only the people who need it to run the service can reach it. Links to order boards, tracking and rider pages are long signed codes that can't be guessed. If something goes wrong that puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

Children

yarnOga is for businesses and adults. Group admins who add a child's birthday and photo for a flyer should have the parent's permission.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change matters, we will say so on this site before it takes effect.

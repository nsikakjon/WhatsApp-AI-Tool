What a WhatsApp AI assistant does

Across Africa, most customers would rather send a WhatsApp message than fill a form or call. That is great for sales, until you are answering the same questions fifty times a day, or a customer messages at midnight and buys from someone else by morning.

An AI assistant sits on your business WhatsApp number and handles those chats for you. Depending on your business, it can:

  • Give prices and take orders. It looks up the exact price on your own price list and the customer replies YES to order. See Quotes & Orders.
  • Send birthday flyers for your group. Church, alumni and company groups get a ready-made flyer every birthday morning. See Birthday Flyers.

Your customers do not install anything. They chat with your number as they always do.

Voice notes and Pidgin

Many customers prefer to talk. A good assistant should understand voice notes, not only typed text. yarnOga understands voice notes in English, Pidgin, Hausa, Swahili and French, and replies in the customer's own language, so nothing is missed.

Prices it never makes up

The biggest risk with AI is a wrong price. In yarnOga, the AI only works out what the customer wants. Every price and total is looked up from your own list and calculated exactly. If something is not on the list, the chat comes to you instead.

WhatsApp Business app or an AI assistant?

The free WhatsApp Business app gives you a catalogue, quick replies and an away message. Those help, but they cannot read a question and work out an answer. An AI assistant runs on Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform, which is built for automated replies.

Good news: if your number is on the WhatsApp Business app, you can keep using the app while the assistant answers alongside you. Or get a new SIM just for orders. We help you choose.

Five questions to ask before you pay anyone

  1. Where do the prices come from?

    They should come from your own list, never from the AI's guess.

  2. Does it use the official WhatsApp platform?

    Unofficial tools can get your number banned. Ask which one they use.

  3. Can it understand voice notes?

    Your customers will send them, so test it with one.

  4. What happens with a question it cannot answer?

    It should pass the chat to you, not invent an answer.

  5. Can I try it before I pay?

    Ask to see it working with your own prices or your own group first.

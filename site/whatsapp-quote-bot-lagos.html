How it works

  • Your own number. Customers message your business number, with your name on it.
  • Your own price list. Send it as a photo, spreadsheet or typed list. Every price the bot gives comes from it.
  • Voice notes and Pidgin. Customers can just talk. The assistant understands and replies in text.
  • Orders to your phone. When a customer replies YES, they get an invoice and you get an alert with the order.

Questions

Things people ask us.

Does it work outside Lagos?

Yes. yarnOga works anywhere in Nigeria and in other African countries, including Ghana, Kenya, Zambia and the CFA franc countries. This page is for Lagos businesses because traffic and late-night orders make fast replies matter even more here.

Will the bot ever quote a wrong price?

No. The AI only works out what the customer wants. Every price and total is looked up from your own price list and calculated exactly. Anything not on your list comes to you.

Can customers send voice notes or write in Pidgin?

Yes. The assistant understands voice notes in English, Pidgin, Hausa, Swahili and French, and replies in the language the customer used.

How much does it cost?

Starter is ₦15,000 a month, or ₦12,750 a month billed yearly, with no setup fee. See all prices.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Message the yarnOga Demo Shop, a made-up shop with a sample price list, and ask for a price like a customer would.

How to add AI to your WhatsApp Business.

You can have an AI assistant answering your customers on WhatsApp, day and night, without learning any technology. Here are the six steps, what each one involves, and the mistakes to avoid.

Read the steps Get it set up for me

The six steps

  1. Decide what the AI should handle

    Write down the five questions customers ask you most. For most businesses it is prices, delivery, opening hours and "is it available?". Start there. You can add more later.

  2. Choose the number

    The assistant needs a number connected to Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. If your number is already on the WhatsApp Business app, you can keep using the app on your phone and the assistant answers alongside you. Or get a new SIM just for orders. Either way, pick a number you will keep for years, because customers will save it.

  3. Connect it to the official platform

    This is done through a Meta Business account and a provider such as yarnOga. Meta checks your business name and approves the display name customers see. Avoid "WhatsApp bots" that run on a phone or a copied app: they break Meta's rules and the number can be banned.

  4. Give the AI your real information

    Send your price list, products and policies. With yarnOga, prices are stored as a list and every total is calculated from it, so the AI never invents a price. Anything not on the list is passed to you.

  5. Test it like a customer

    Message it from your own phone. Ask in Pidgin, make a spelling mistake, send a voice note, ask for something you don't sell. A good assistant understands all of these and hands over what it can't answer.

  6. Tell your customers

    Put the number on your flyers, receipts, Instagram bio, Google Business Profile and shop sign. Add a wa.me link with your country code (for example wa.me/234XXXXXXXXXX in Nigeria or wa.me/254XXXXXXXXX in Kenya) so people can tap to chat. Our free link generator makes one for you.

What it costs

Meta does not charge for replies to customers who message you first. It charges for messages your business starts, such as marketing broadcasts. On top of that you pay your provider.

yarnOga's Quotes & Orders assistant is from ₦15,000 a month (₦12,750 a month billed yearly) with no setup fee, and outside Nigeria you pay a set price in your own currency. See Quotes & Orders.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Letting the AI quote prices from memory. Prices must come from your list.
  • No way to reach a person. Some customers need you. Make sure the chat can come to you.
  • Using your only personal number. You lose it in the normal app.

Questions

Common questions.

Can I add AI to the normal WhatsApp Business app?

Not inside the app itself. The app has quick replies and an away message, but a real AI assistant needs Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. With yarnOga you can connect the same number and keep using the app alongside the assistant.

Will I lose my chats?

No. If your number is on the WhatsApp Business app, you connect it with Facebook and keep using the app, chats included, while the assistant answers alongside you. Or use a new SIM just for orders.

How long does it take?

With yarnOga you can try your own bot the same day: send sign up, send your prices and test it. Connecting your number with Facebook takes a few minutes.

Can customers send voice notes?

Yes. yarnOga understands voice notes in English, Pidgin, Hausa, Swahili and French, and replies in the language the customer used.
